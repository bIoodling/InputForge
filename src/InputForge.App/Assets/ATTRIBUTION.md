# Xbox Elite Controller

**Xbox Elite Controller** by [Rusfort](https://sketchfab.com/Rusfort), from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/xbox-elite-controller-32aad0ff25f7446b8afe649ea3cc9e43).

At runtime InputForge centres and scales the model, divides existing triangles into selectable surfaces, links control transforms to real controller state, and maps the original base and emissive atlases in fixed UV space. WPF uses a specular approximation derived from the original metallic and roughness channels. Tangent-space normal mapping and physical transmission are unsupported in this viewer.

The creator describes the model as an interpretation with proportions that differ from the physical controller. Xbox names and marks identify the represented hardware. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
